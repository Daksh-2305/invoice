### ✨ Key Design Upgrades:
1.  **Bottom Navigation Bar**: One-thumb access to the most used tools.
2.  **Primary FAB (+)**: The large center button creates an invoice in one tap.
3.  **Intelligent Cards**: No more horizontal scrolling. Tables turn into clean cards that show exactly what you need.
4.  **Extreme Glassmorphism**: Updated borders and blurs for a "SaaS 2026" premium feel.

> [!TIP]
> This redesign makes the app feel like a **native iOS/Android app** while staying 100% in the browser.
